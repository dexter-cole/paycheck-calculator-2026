# Paycheck Calculator 2026

A free 2026 take-home pay calculator for every U.S. state, built by [PaycheckZip](https://paycheckzip.com).

Federal withholding follows IRS Publication 15-T, with FICA and a cited state line on each page.

## What you'll find

- State-by-state paycheck calculators with federal, FICA and state tax lines
- A [bonus tax calculator](https://paycheckzip.com/bonus-tax-calculator)
- A guide to [whether your city taxes your paycheck](https://paycheckzip.com/does-my-city-tax-my-paycheck)
- Notes on the [earned income credit](https://paycheckzip.com/earned-income-credit)
